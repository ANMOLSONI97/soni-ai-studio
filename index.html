    A few useful answers

    Clear from the start.

    What does AI-assisted mean?

    AI tools can help with ideas, drafts and parts of a workflow. People still need to review the output, check accuracy and decide what is ready to use. It does not mean an autonomous business or guaranteed results.

    Can you work with an existing website?

    An existing website can be reviewed before deciding whether to improve it or rebuild. The right approach depends on its platform, access, content and what you want to achieve.

    How much does a project cost?

    Pricing depends on the agreed scope, integrations and support needs. A project quote should clearly separate build work from hosting, domains, AI usage and other third-party costs. No prices or packages are committed on this page.

    Is this website running a live AI assistant?

    No. This is a service website. The project planner uses fixed templates in your browser. It does not send your answers to an AI model, submit an enquiry or create an account.

    What about business data and privacy?

    Start with non-sensitive examples. Any proposed AI integration should be reviewed for access permissions, data use, retention and the provider's terms before customer or business data is shared. This page has no analytics, tracking scripts or enquiry database. WhatsApp and email links open external services, where their own privacy policies apply.
